Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

In short

  • The Statut app collects no personal data. Your information stays on your iPhone.
  • No account, no ads, no trackers, no analytics.
  • This website keeps your email only if you sign up to test the app.

The Statut app

What is stored, and where

Your profile (status, passport country, school, province) and your document dates. They are stored only on your iPhone and encrypted by iOS when the device is locked. No server receives them.

What is never asked for

No permit, passport or SIN number: only dates. When you scan a document, the photo is read on your iPhone to find the date, then forgotten. It is neither stored nor sent.

Reminders

They are local notifications, scheduled on your iPhone. With “Discreet reminders”, the document name and date don’t appear on the Lock Screen.

Rules updates

At most once a day, the app downloads the public rules (deadlines, countries, embassies, schools) from this website. No information about you is sent. As with any web page, the host sees the connection’s IP address. The files are signed: the app rejects any version that doesn’t come from Statut.

Backups

If iCloud or computer backup is turned on for your iPhone, Statut’s data is included, like your other apps. That backup is tied to your Apple Account, not to Statut.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings, then Delete everything. Or delete the app. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

This website

If you sign up to test the app, we keep your email and your phone type (iPhone or Android), only to send you the app link and the launch announcement. We don’t sell or share them. To be removed from the list, reply to any of our emails or write to statut.app@icloud.com.

The website uses no advertising cookies or audience measurement, and loads no fonts or scripts from other sites.

Third-party services

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and the rule files.
  • Google Sheets stores the test sign-up list, in a private spreadsheet.
  • Apple distributes the app (App Store, TestFlight) under its own privacy terms.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with its effective date.
